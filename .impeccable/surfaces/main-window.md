# Anzu main window

Mode: Operate. The user comes to convert their books; the screen is a tool.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A sober working console: the book table is the screen. It rejects the grid of
identical cards and the panel of three big metrics that this kind of app usually
carries.

OWN-WORLD: “Amber console”, chosen by the user. It contributes four things and only
those: monospaced type (JetBrains Mono), high density, an ink palette with amber as
the only accent, and one trait of its own. Nearly square corners, thin lines, no
shadows. Light theme “Cold paper”: cold white background #F3F5F7, white panels, ink
#14171A, toasted amber #B25E00; amber is never a background. Dark theme: warm charcoal
#0E0F0C with amber #FFB000. The header is a band of dark ink in both themes.

STORY: The user sees whether the system is ready, recognises their books by title,
ticks the ones they want and presses a button that says what is going to happen.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Header band with logo, theme and “Check again”. Below it, a status
line. Then the table, which takes up the rest: selection, title, ASIN, size and
status, with sortable headers. At the bottom, destination folder and the action.
Everything in a centred column of at most 1160 px.

SIGNATURE: Each book's status is a three-stage track, with DRM → unlocked → EPUB, that
fills in as it goes.

REFUSALS: No terminal costume (brackets, pseudo-keys, command prompt). Standard
controls. Drawn icons, not text glyphs.
